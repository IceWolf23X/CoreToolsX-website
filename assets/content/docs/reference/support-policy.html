Release channels

CoreToolsX is free to download and use. Public stable releases are distributed through Modrinth. Paid memberships do not sell the stable plugin.

Supporter preview builds

Supporters may receive temporary previews, release candidates, staging builds and non-critical hotfix candidates for testing. They are not supporter-exclusive stable releases.

Critical fixes

Confirmed security, exploit, data-loss, major-crash and other critical stability fixes are released publicly when ready.

Paid memberships

Membership may include priority support, setup/configuration review, troubleshooting, debug/log analysis, preview channels, roadmap updates and testing participation. It does not unlock in-game functionality.

Discord authentication

The bot verifies active Patreon membership and assigns Discord roles. The plugin does not require Patreon authentication.
