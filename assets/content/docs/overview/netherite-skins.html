Skin recipes accept clean netherite tools and convert them to a vanilla visual material while applying netherite-equivalent tool/weapon components. Already-skinned items must be stripped first.

Default skins

Wood, stone, copper, iron, gold and diamond skins cover sword, pickaxe, axe, shovel, hoe and spear.

Inventory model modes

VISUAL_SKIN is the default: inventory and hand show the visual material and no resource pack is required. REAL_TIER assigns item_model: coretoolsx:skinned/<material_tool> and requires the separate addon so GUI/ground/fixed/shelf contexts show netherite while the hand retains the visual skin.

Lore

skins.lore.generated-lines controls final lore. A standalone {item_lore} entry reinserts the saved original lore. Other placeholders cover skin identity, clean/visual/real tier and cosmetic durability.

Cosmetic durability

Modes are DISABLED, GLOBAL, BY_SKIN and BY_ITEM. Damage currently mirrors real item damage. Break action is REMOVE_SKIN; mending can be DISABLED, SPLIT or DOUBLE.

Removal and fire

By default, a skinned tool plus shears returns clean netherite and damages the shears by one. Dropped skinned tools are protected from configured fire/lava destruction while stripping is disabled; when enabled, those causes strip the skin.
