Each child of upgrades is an independent route.

Definition schema

  • enabled.
  • from-tier or exact from-material.
  • to-tier or exact to-material.
  • ingredient: Bukkit material or configured group.
  • cost-mode: VANILLA_RECIPE_COST|CUSTOM.
  • Positive count for a custom cost.
  • permission when locks apply.

Bundled definitions

wood_to_stone, stone_to_copper, copper_to_iron, iron_to_gold and iron_to_diamond. Supported tiers are wood, stone, copper, iron, gold, diamond and netherite.

Validation

Missing endpoints, unknown materials/groups and invalid costs disable the affected definition with a warning.
